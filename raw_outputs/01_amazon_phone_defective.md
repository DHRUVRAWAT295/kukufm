**User:** Screen stopped working after 10 days. Paid Rs 22,000. I complained to Amazon on 1 April 2026, they said contact the seller. I live in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.

**Bot:** **Where you stand**

You bought a defective phone on Amazon for Rs 22000 on 2026-03-15. You complained on 2026-04-01, but Amazon told you to contact the seller. Under "Rule 4(5) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020", the platform has a duty to address consumer grievances. Your next step is to send a formal notice. In this notice, you can ask them to replace the product or refund the full price. If they do not resolve the issue, you can approach the consumer forum. Under "the Consumer Protection (Jurisdiction) Rules, 2021", your case goes to the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission. Under "Section 35 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019", you can file the complaint yourself without a lawyer. Under "Section 39 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019", the commission can order a replacement or refund. Under "Section 69 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019", you have two years to file.

**Your next step:** The platform's one-month period has passed. The next step is a legal notice to the seller and the platform.

**Where a complaint would go:** District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission for Varanasi. You can file online at e-jagriti.gov.in.

**To draft your legal notice I need a few details.**

1. What is your full name?
2. What is your postal address? It goes at the top of the notice.
3. What is the seller's name? It's usually printed on the invoice. Say 'not sure' if you can't find it.
